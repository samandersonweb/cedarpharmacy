Our Story & Community Purpose

Healthcare rooted in community, care, and uncompromising standards.

Cedar Pharmacy was founded to bring heartfelt, independent pharmacy care back to Minneapolis. We believe your pharmacist should know your name, understand your health history, and advocate for your wellness.

The Cedar Philosophy

A healthcare partner who takes the time to listen.

In a landscape increasingly dominated by massive corporate chains with long lines, disconnected call centers, and rushed interactions, Cedar Pharmacy is proudly different.

Located conveniently on Cedar Avenue in Minneapolis, we combine cutting-edge pharmaceutical dispensing technology with old-fashioned neighborhood hospitality. When you have questions regarding medication side effects, interactions, or insurance coverage, our pharmacists take the time to explain everything clearly.

How We Serve You

Our Patient-Focused Care Model

Everything at Cedar Pharmacy is designed to make managing your health seamless, stress-free, and affordable.

01

Prescription Synchronization

Tired of multiple trips to the pharmacy every month? We align all of your monthly refills so you can pick them up on one single scheduled date every 30 or 90 days.

02

Direct Doctor Coordination

When a prescription needs an authorized refill or prior authorization from your doctor, our staff directly contacts your clinic so you don't have to spend hours on hold.

03

Multilingual Patient Support

Cedar Ave is home to a rich and vibrant community. We take pride in offering patient consultations and instruction in multiple languages to ensure every patient feels comfortable.
