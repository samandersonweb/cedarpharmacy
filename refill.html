Help & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions About Refills

What happens if my prescription has zero refills remaining?

If your prescription is out of refills, go ahead and submit this form anyway! Our team will contact your clinic or healthcare prescriber electronically to request a renewal authorization on your behalf.

Can someone else pick up my prescription for me?

Yes, a family member or caregiver may pick up your medication. In compliance with Minnesota pharmacy law, they will be asked to confirm the patient’s full name, date of birth, and home address at pickup.

How do I transfer a prescription from another pharmacy to Cedar?

Transferring is simple and seamless. Call us at (612) 354-3851 or send us a message via our Contact page with your current pharmacy's name and the medication name. We handle the entire transfer from your previous pharmacy.
